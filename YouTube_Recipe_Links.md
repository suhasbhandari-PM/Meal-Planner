# YouTube Recipe Links for the Recipe Bank

Companion to `Healthy_Recipe_Bank.md`. Recipe IDs (B1, L4, S3, etc.) match that file.

**How to use these**
- I picked links from YouTube search results by title and description. I have **not watched them**, so quality varies.
- Most YouTube versions use more oil, salt, butter, cheese or cream than your plan allows. Watch for the method, then apply the house rules from the recipe bank: ½ tsp salt for the whole dish, 1 tsp oil per person, no cheese or cream, no chaat masala.
- Where a video is a close but not exact match, I say so.

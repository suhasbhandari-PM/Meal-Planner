Meal Roller

Each slot has its own Reroll button, so you can keep what you like (Lock) and reroll the rest. Each result comes with video and recipe links.

Filters

Nutrition numbers are per adult serving and are estimates (about ±15%). The video links come from YouTube search results and may use more oil, salt or butter than your plan, so cut those back. "Web search" opens a Google search for a written recipe. Both adults should keep salt under 5 g a day, and follow the doctor's advice for hypertension.
